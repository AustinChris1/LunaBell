# LunaBell demo video: production brief

Follow this top to bottom. Every scene says what is on screen, what to do, and
the exact words to say. You are recording with Recordly on the laptop and the
phone's built in screen recorder on the phone, then joining the two in an editor.

**Final video target:** 2 minutes 30 seconds, 1920 x 1080, MP4, captions burned in.
Judges score functionality at 45 of 100 points and the validator requires a public
YouTube, Loom or Vimeo link, so this video has one job: prove the bell rings on a
real payment, on camera, with no cuts across the critical moment.

---

## 1. What LunaBell is, in two sentences

LunaBell is a payment terminal you listen to instead of watch. You name an amount,
show a QR code, and when the payment settles on the Nimiq chain your phone or laptop
chimes and speaks the amount out loud, then gives you a receipt that re-reads the
chain every time it is opened, so a screenshot can never fake it.

---

## 2. What you need before recording

### Devices

- [ ] **Laptop** at 1920 x 1080. This is the till. Browser zoom 125% so the app reads
  large on video. Speakers on and audible. This is where the bell rings.
- [ ] **Phone** with **Nimiq Pay** installed and signed in. This is the payer.
- [ ] Both on the same table so you can film the phone approaching the laptop later
  if you want a live shot; not required.

### Money

- [ ] **Some NIM in the phone's Nimiq Pay wallet, on mainnet.** LunaBell charges
  **5 NIM** per take. Fees on NIM are zero. Two rehearsals plus one take is 15 NIM.
  Keep **20 NIM** so you are not sweating the balance. At current prices that is
  well under a cent. You can **buy a tiny amount in Pay**, receive it from a friend,
  or move it from wallet.nimiq.com. Testnet NIM is only for practice. **The submitted
  video must be a real mainnet payment.**
- [ ] Your **Nimiq Pay receiving address** copied to the laptop clipboard. In Pay: tap
  Receive, copy the NQ address. You will paste it into LunaBell's Pay to field on the
  laptop, so the laptop rings when the phone pays it.

### Software

- [ ] **Recordly** on the laptop. Settings: 1920 x 1080, 30 fps, **system audio ON**
  (the bell must be captured), microphone ON for narration, cursor visible, click
  highlights off.
- [ ] **Phone screen recording** (iOS Control Centre, or Android quick settings). Turn
  the phone's microphone recording off; you only need its screen.
- [ ] **CapCut Desktop** (free) or DaVinci Resolve to join the two recordings and add
  captions.
- [ ] A quiet room. Hiss or echo makes the spoken amount unintelligible and that is
  the whole product.

### Pre loaded state

- [ ] Laptop browser: https://lunabell.vercel.app open in a single tab, light theme,
  no other tabs, bookmarks bar hidden.
- [ ] LunaBell **Today** tally cleared (open /app, expand Today, press Clear) so the
  first ring in the video is the first row.
- [ ] Nimiq Pay language set to English so the spoken amount matches the captions.
- [ ] Phone notifications silenced.

## 3. Rehearse the critical moment first, off camera

Do this twice before any recording. If it fails, stop and message me.

1. Laptop: open https://lunabell.vercel.app/app. Type `5` on the pad. Memo `Two coffees`.
   Paste your Pay address into **Pay to**. The identicon appears. Press **Listen**.
2. Phone: open the **camera app** (not Nimiq Pay), point it at the QR on the laptop,
   tap the link. A LunaBell page opens in the phone browser showing 5 NIM.
   If the camera does not offer a link, on the laptop press **Share**, copy the URL,
   and open it in the phone browser.
3. Tap **Open in Nimiq Pay**. Nimiq Pay opens with the same page inside it and a gold
   **Pay 5 NIM** button. Tap it. Confirm in the wallet sheet. The first time, Pay may
   warn that the host is not in the directory yet. Proceed.
4. The laptop polls the chain every 3.5 seconds. Expect the chime and "Received 5 NIM"
   within about **four to ten seconds**, then a green paid screen.

If step 4 does not happen within fifteen seconds, do not record. Send me a screenshot of
both screens.

## 4. Before pressing record

1. Close everything except the LunaBell tab. Do Not Disturb on.
2. Recordly: confirm system audio is being captured. Press Test the bell in LunaBell
   and record five seconds. Play it back. You must hear the chime and the voice
   clearly. If you only hear your microphone, fix the system audio setting first.
3. Phone: start the screen recording just before Scene 4 and stop it after Scene 5.
4. Read every line 20% slower than feels natural.

## 5. The script

Seven scenes. Read the words exactly. If you fumble, pause one second and restart the
sentence; you will cut it later.

### Scene 1: The bell, before anything (0:00 to 0:20)

**Show:** https://lunabell.vercel.app landing page, top of the page. The live block
number is ticking in the header.

**Do:** Click **Hear the bell**. Let the chime and the voice finish. Do not talk over it.

**Say (after the voice finishes):**

> That is LunaBell. A payment terminal you listen to instead of watch. It only makes
> that sound when money has actually arrived on the Nimiq chain.

---

### Scene 2: The problem (0:20 to 0:40)

**Show:** Scroll slowly to the section with the three big numbers. Let them count up.

**Do:** Stop on the merchant quote.

**Say:**

> The fake payment screenshot is one of the oldest tricks against anyone who sells
> in person. Someone shows you a sent screen, walks off, nothing ever arrives. India
> answered it with hardware. Paytm and PhonePe shipped over nine million soundboxes
> whose only job is to shout when a payment is real. LunaBell is that device as
> software, inside Nimiq Pay.

---

### Scene 3: Set up the charge (0:40 to 1:00)

**Show:** Click **Open in browser**. The till appears with the keypad.

**Do:** Tap `5`. Type `Two coffees` in the memo. Paste your Pay address into
**Pay to**; the identicon appears. Press **Listen**. The QR appears with the Nimiq
hexagon in the middle and "Listening, block ..." underneath.

**Say:**

> Five NIM for two coffees. Press Listen. LunaBell is now watching the chain for one
> specific payment: this exact amount, to this address, carrying this charge's own tag
> in the transaction data. Anything else stays silent.

---

### Scene 4: The payment, no cuts (1:00 to 1:35)

**This is the scene the whole video exists for. One continuous laptop recording.
No cut between pressing Pay and hearing the bell.**

**Show:** Laptop stays on the listening screen. Phone screen recording starts now.

**Do, on the phone, slowly:**

1. Open the camera. Point at the laptop QR. Tap the link that appears.
2. The LunaBell pay page opens in the phone browser: 5 NIM, Two coffees, the
   recipient identicon.
3. Tap **Open in Nimiq Pay**. Nimiq Pay opens with the same page and a gold
   **Pay 5 NIM** button.
4. Tap **Pay 5 NIM**. Confirm in the wallet.
5. **Put the phone down and look at the laptop.** Say nothing. Let the laptop chime and
   speak "Received 5 NIM". The screen turns green with the amount.

**Say (start before you pick up the phone, finish before you tap Pay, then stay
silent until the bell has spoken):**

> The customer scans it with any camera and pays from their own wallet. LunaBell
> never touches the money. Watch the laptop.

**Editing note:** keep the laptop recording continuous from Pay to the bell. Show the
phone recording as a picture in picture in the bottom right during steps 1 to 4, then
cut the phone window away the moment you tap Pay so the viewer's eyes are on the
laptop when it rings. Do not speed this up. Do not add music over the bell.

---

### Scene 5: The receipt that keeps counting (1:35 to 2:05)

**Show:** The green paid screen on the laptop with From and To identicons.

**Do:**

1. Press **Replay**. Let it speak again.
2. Press **Receipt**. The receipt page loads: verified against the chain, block,
   confirmations.
3. Wait three seconds, then **reload the page**. The confirmation number is higher.
   Hold on it for two full seconds.

**Say:**

> Every ring is recorded, and every receipt is a link, not a picture. Watch the
> confirmations. I reload. The number went up, because this page reads the chain
> every time anyone opens it. A screenshot cannot do that. Forward this link into any
> "I sent it" argument and it settles itself.

**Editing note:** a slow 1.1x zoom on the confirmations row across the reload is the
second most important two seconds of the video.

---

### Scene 6: The rest of the day (2:05 to 2:20)

**Show:** Press the browser back button to the till, then **New charge**.

**Do:** Under the keypad, the **Today** panel shows 1 sale, 5 NIM. Expand it. Press
**Say the total**. Let it speak. Tap the repeat arrow; the pad reloads 5 and the memo.

**Say:**

> At the end of the day LunaBell tells you what you took, out loud. One tap repeats a
> charge. It speaks five languages, follows your wallet's setting, installs to the
> home screen, and any other Mini App can open it with a charge already filled in.

---

### Scene 7: Close (2:20 to 2:30)

**Show:** Toggle the theme to dark on the till, hold two seconds, then cut to the
thumbnail image (submission/thumbnail.png). Hold five seconds, silent.

**Say (before the cut):**

> LunaBell. The bell that only rings when the lunas are real. NIM native, open source,
> built for Nimiq Pay.

## 6. Editing checklist

**Audio**
- [ ] Do not touch the bell. No music under Scene 4 or under any spoken amount.
- [ ] Ambient instrumental at -25 dB under Scenes 1 to 3 and 6 only.
- [ ] Remove silences over half a second, except the deliberate ones in Scene 4 and 5.
- [ ] Normalise voice to -3 dB peak. No AI voice enhancement.

**Captions**
- [ ] Auto captions, then proofread every line. Watch for: Nimiq (NIM-ick), NIM,
  luna, lunas, LunaBell, Paytm, PhonePe, Nimiq Pay.
- [ ] Caption the spoken amount exactly as the app says it: "Received 5 NIM".
- [ ] 32 pt bold sans serif, white with black shadow, bottom centre, burned in.

**Visuals**
- [ ] Hard cuts only. One fade to the end card.
- [ ] Two zooms only: the green paid screen when the bell rings, and the confirmations
  row across the reload.
- [ ] Phone recording as picture in picture during Scene 4 steps 1 to 4, removed the
  instant you tap Pay.
- [ ] No filters or grading.

**Open and close**
- [ ] Open cold on the landing page, no title card. The first thing the viewer hears
  is the bell.
- [ ] Close on submission/thumbnail.png for five seconds.

## 8. If something goes wrong

Stop and send me a screenshot of both screens if:

- The laptop does not ring within ten seconds of the wallet confirming.
- The phone's **Open in Nimiq Pay** button does nothing or opens a directory error.
- Nimiq Pay shows the pay page but no gold Pay button (it means the page is not
  running inside Pay).
- The receipt shows "Could not reach the chain".

Do not troubleshoot on camera. A clean rehearsal is worth more than a fixed take.

---

## Runtime check

Scenes add to 2:30. Under 2:15 feels thin; over 3:00 loses judges. If Scene 4 runs
long because the chain took a moment, keep it. That wait is the honesty of the video.
