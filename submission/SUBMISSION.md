---

LunaBell is the bell that only rings when the lunas are real.

Someone says they paid. Their screen says sent. You still do not know if the money arrived. LunaBell does. Name an amount, show the QR, put the phone down. When the payment settles on Nimiq, the phone chimes and speaks the amount. Then you get a receipt that is a link, not a picture: every time anyone opens it, it re-reads the chain, so its confirmation count keeps climbing. A screenshot cannot do that.

India answered fake payment screenshots with hardware. Paytm and PhonePe have deployed over nine million soundboxes. LunaBell is that device as software, inside a wallet already in people's pockets, for a stall, a cafe, a freelancer, or a roommate who says "I sent it."

A charge lives entirely in its own link. The payer sends a tagged NIM transaction. LunaBell matches recipient, exact amount, tag and block window, and rings only then. No database, no account, nothing custodial.

MIT. NIM native. Built to be trusted with your hands full.
